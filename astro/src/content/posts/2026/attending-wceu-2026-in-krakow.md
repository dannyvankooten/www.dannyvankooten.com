Next week, I’ll be attending [WordCamp Europe](https://europe.wordcamp.org/2026/) again for the first time [since 2014](/blog/2014/2014-year-in-review/). I’m looking forward to reconnecting with people from the WordPress community and hopefully meeting some new faces too.

I’m not going to fill my calendar with back-to-back meetings. I’ve never really enjoyed that approach to conferences, and some of the best conversations happen spontaneously.

That said, if you’ll be in Krakow and would like to make sure we get a chance to chat, feel free to [get in touch](/contact/).

## Hitting the trails on Sunday

After the event wraps up, I’ll be renting mountain bikes on Sunday to explore the trails west of Krakow.

If you enjoy riding bikes, being outdoors, and occasionally dodging trees, you’re welcome to join. Nothing competitive. Just a fun ride and a good way to unwind after a few days of conference talks and conversations.

If that sounds like your kind of thing, let me know via email, Mastodon, or Twitter.
